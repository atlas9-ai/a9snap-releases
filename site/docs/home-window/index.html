    Manual · Settings

    The Home window

    The one window A9 Snap has: your actions on the left, your newest captures in the middle, and every setting a click away.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.3.2

    Open the Home window by double-clicking the tray icon, or by choosing Settings in the tray menu. It always opens on Home, however you left it. Closing the window does not quit A9 Snap: it keeps waiting in the tray.

    The left side#

    • Capture starts a screenshot, just like the hotkey. Your capture hotkey shows on the button.
    • Recent captures opens the strip. Its hotkey shows on the button.
    • Editor opens your newest capture in the editor. It is dimmed until you have a capture.
    • Folder opens your screenshots folder in Explorer.
    • The menu of sections has Home, Shortcuts, Saving, Strip and editor, Notifications and Updates and about. Click one, or use the up and down arrow keys. See Settings sections.
    • When an update is waiting, a card appears with Update now. It restarts A9 Snap and takes a few seconds.
    • At the bottom is the version and channel, for example v0.3.2 · Dev, with Up to date beside it when that is so.

    The middle#

    • Ready when you are reminds you of the hotkey and shows Running in the tray.
    • Recent captures shows your six newest captures as thumbnails, with the file name, how long ago you took each, and a Volt dot on edited ones. Click one to open it in the editor. Open the strip shows more. With no captures yet it says where to start.
    • Saving to shows your screenshots folder and Next file, the name the next capture will get.
    • After a capture says what happens when you let go: Copied as an image, Copied as image and file, and Opens in the editor if you turned that on.

    The size of the window#

    It opens at 1000 × 680 and can be made as small as 860 × 600. At the smallest size the right-hand side scrolls.
