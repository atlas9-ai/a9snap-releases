Every capture is also saved in one folder, numbered in order, so the newest is always on top and nothing gets lost.

    Questions

    Short answers.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Capture, the loupe, numbered files, recent captures and the editor are free for good. No subscription, ever.

    Does it send my screenshots anywhere?

    No. Files go to the folder you pick. The app sends nothing beyond a check for updates and, only if you buy Pro, its license check. This website counts visits and download clicks without cookies. Privacy has the details.

    Is it safe to install?

    Yes. Early builds aren’t signed with a Windows certificate yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. The Microsoft Store version avoids that step. There’s no account, and the app sends nothing beyond the update check (and Pro’s license check, if you buy Pro). The manual shows each screen.

    Two monitors? 150% scaling?

    Yes. Every monitor is captured at its real resolution, and the selection lands on the pixels you see.

    Can I change Alt+Z?

    Yes, in Settings. If another app already owns a shortcut, A9 Snap tells you instead of failing quietly. Shortcuts in the manual lists every key.

    Mac or Linux?

    Windows 10 and 11 for now.

    Where is the manual?

    At a9snap.com/docs: how to capture, the recent captures strip, the editor, every setting, and what to do if something goes wrong. It has a search box, and the app’s Home window links to it.
