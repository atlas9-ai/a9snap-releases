    Auto-redact

    Auto-redact finds email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers in a picture and blurs them, so you can send it without a second look at every line. It is a Pro feature, and it reads the picture on your PC.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.9.0

    Screenshots catch more than you meant them to: an email address in a corner, a phone number in a signature, a card number in a receipt. Auto-redact is part of A9 Snap Pro. It reads the words in the picture with the same Windows text recognition that Copy text and Alt+X use, finds the ones that look like private details and blurs them. Nothing is uploaded: the reading happens on your PC.

    What it finds#

    • Email addresses, such as ana.lee+news@mail.example.co.uk.
    • Phone numbers in the usual North American writings, (555) 123-4567, 555-123-4567, 555.123.4567, 1 555 123 4567, and international numbers that start with a plus, +44 20 7183 8750. A number split across words is still one number.
    • Card numbers: 13 to 19 digits that pass the card check, written together or in groups split by spaces or dashes.

    Two more are in the list and off until you turn them on in Saving: IP addresses and long key-like strings (a long run of letters and digits with no spaces, the shape of an access key).

    Look-alikes are left alone: a date such as 2024-10-31, a version such as 1.2.3, an order number, a sum, a number that fails the card check. A phone number must be written with a space, dash, dot or bracket, or start with a plus, so a plain run of ten digits (an id, a time stamp) is not blurred.

    Windows has to read the text correctly for A9 Snap to find it. Small, blurry or stylised text may be missed, and a misread digit can turn a card number into one that fails the check. Look at the picture before you send it. Auto-redact is a safety net, not a guarantee.

    In the editor#

    Open a capture in the editor and press R, or click Redact in the top bar. A9 Snap reads the capture and puts a Blur over every match, a few pixels larger than the words. A line under the toolbar says what it did: Blurred 3 items: 2 emails, 1 phone number. When nothing is found it says Nothing to redact found., and pressing it again on a picture that is already blurred says Already blurred.

    The blurs are ordinary Blur shapes. You can select one, move it, resize it, delete it with Delete, or undo the whole Redact with Ctrl+Z, and you can add your own. A blur is real pixelation: once you save, the file holds flat blocks where the text was. See Blur is permanent in the saved file.

    Without Pro the Redact button shows a Pro badge and opens the Pro sheet.

    In the strip#

    Right-click a capture in the strip and choose Copy redacted. A blurred copy of the picture goes on the clipboard, ready to paste. The file is not changed. The note says Copied redacted · 3 blurred, or Copied · nothing to redact found when there was nothing to blur (the picture is then copied as it is). Without Pro the item shows a Pro badge and opens the Pro sheet.

    Redact every capture#

    Turn on Redact every capture before it's copied in Saving and every new capture is read and blurred before it reaches the clipboard and your screenshots folder. It is off by default, and it needs Pro.

    • It adds a moment. The picture is on the clipboard only once Windows has read it: about a second for a full screen of text, a fraction of that for a small region. A capture with nothing to blur is copied as it is.
    • The untouched original is kept, in the hidden .a9snap folder next to your screenshots, like an edit's original. Open the capture in the editor and Revert brings it back; Clear on the Edit originals row in Saving removes it, after which the capture can no longer be reverted. The capture shows the Volt dot of an edited one. Delete .a9snap before you share the whole folder.
    • If Windows can't read the picture (no text recognition language is installed, say), the capture is copied and saved as it is, and the note says couldn't check for private text.
    • Secure captures are never redacted. They stay out of the strip and the screenshots folder already, and they are never read.

    What auto-redact blurs in the same section chooses the kinds, for this setting, Redact in the editor and Copy redacted in the strip.

    Without Pro#

    Without Pro, Redact and Copy redacted show a Pro badge and open the Pro sheet, and Redact every capture is greyed out. Nothing is read and the license is not checked: a free copy never makes one.
