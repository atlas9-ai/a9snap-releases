    Questions

    Short answers.

    Will capture ever cost money?

    No. Capture, the loupe, numbered files and the editor are free for good. That’s in writing below.

    Is it safe to install?

    Yes. Early builds aren’t signed with a Windows certificate yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway. Signed builds arrive with the Store release. There’s no account, and nothing leaves your PC except the update check.

    Does it send my screenshots anywhere?

    No. Files go to the folder you pick. The only thing Snap sends is a check for updates.

    Two monitors? 150% scaling?

    Yes. Every monitor is captured at its real resolution, and the selection lands on the pixels you see.

    Can I change Alt+Z?

    Yes, in Settings. If another app already owns a shortcut, Snap tells you instead of failing quietly.

    What’s in Pro?

    Planned: copy the text out of a capture, and smart file names. $29 once, yours to keep. Capture never moves behind it.

    Mac or Linux?

    Windows 10 and 11 for now.

    The Capture Pledge

    Capture will never be a subscription.

    Not now, not after we’re popular, not in a “legacy plan.” If we ever charge for something new, you’ll pay for it once and own it.
