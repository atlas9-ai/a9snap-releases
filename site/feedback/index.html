Suggest a feature.

One email is all it takes. Three things help most:

  • What you were trying to do. The job you had in front of you.
  • What got in the way. The step that was slow, confusing or missing.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Attach it to the email, and check it for private details first.

Email your suggestion
