    Keyboard shortcuts

    Every key A9 Snap uses, what each does, its default, and where you can change it.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.3.4

    A9 Snap has two kinds of keys. Global hotkeys work from any app, even when no A9 Snap window is open. You can change these. Window keys work only while the strip or the editor is in front. These are fixed.

    Keys are written like Alt+Z, which means hold Alt and press Z.

    Global hotkeys#

    Change both in Shortcuts.

    KeyDefaultWhat it does
    Capture hotkeyAlt+ZFreezes the screen and lets you drag a region.
    Recent captures hotkeyAlt+Shift+ZOpens the strip. Press it again to close the strip.

    While you pick a region#

    KeyWhat it does
    EscCancels. Nothing is copied or saved.
    Right-clickCancels.
    Click without draggingCancels, so an accidental hotkey costs nothing.

    In the recent captures strip#

    See The recent captures strip.

    KeyWhat it does
    1 to 9Picks or unpicks that capture.
    Arrow keys, Home, EndMove the focus ring.
    SpacePicks or unpicks the focused capture.
    Ctrl+APicks all of them.
    Enter or Ctrl+CCopies the picked captures, or the focused one if none are picked.
    EOpens the picked capture, or the focused one, in the editor.
    DeleteMoves the picked captures, or the focused one, to the Recycle Bin. The note has an Undo button.
    EscCloses the strip.

    In the Home window#

    KeyWhat it does
    ↑ and ↓Move through the menu of sections, once a section in the menu has the focus.
    DeleteOn a capture in Recent captures: moves it to the Recycle Bin. The note at the bottom has an Undo button.
    F1Opens the manual page for the section on screen, in your browser.

    Keys A9 Snap never takes#

    A hotkey needs a modifier (Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win), except function keys and PrintScreen. A9 Snap refuses these, because they belong to Windows or to every app's editing keys:

    • Win+Shift+S, Windows' own screen snipping.
    • Ctrl+Alt+S.
    • Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+Z.

    The capture hotkey and the recent captures hotkey also can't be the same key.
