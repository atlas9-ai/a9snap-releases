    Share to your apps

    Send a capture to the app you work in with one click, through Windows' own Share sheet. A9 Snap needs no account, no connection and makes no network call for it. It is a Pro feature.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.13.0

    After a capture you usually paste it into a chat, an email or a ticket. Share opens the Windows Share sheet on the capture, so any app that accepts shares is one click away: mail, chat, team apps, nearby sharing and whatever else you have installed. It is part of A9 Snap Pro: see Pro features.

    Where to find Share#

    • On the confirmation. After a capture, the note at the bottom right has Share next to Edit. A recording's note has it next to Save as GIF and Show in folder.
    • In the strip. Pick a capture (or several) and press H, or right-click one and choose Share…. A recording and a GIF have it too.
    • In the editor. Click Share in the top bar. If you changed the picture, A9 Snap saves it over the capture, the same way Done does, and then opens the Share sheet on the saved file; if you changed nothing, the capture goes as it is and nothing is written. The editor stays open.

    Without Pro these buttons carry a small PRO badge, and clicking one opens the Pro sheet instead. A secure capture has no Share button anywhere.

    What the apps get#

    A picture goes as a file and, for apps that take pictures, as the picture itself. A recording (.mp4) or a GIF goes as a file. Pick several captures in the strip and press H, and they go together as files.

    Windows decides what the sheet lists. It already puts the apps you used last at the top, and Email is among the targets, so A9 Snap keeps no list of its own.

    Privacy#

    A9 Snap hands the file to Windows and does nothing else: no account, no connection, no link, no upload, and no network call of its own. When you pick an app in the sheet, that app takes the file and does what it does; A9 Snap never sees where it went. Free never uses Share, and the log says only that the sheet opened for a number of files, never their names. See Privacy.

    If the sheet doesn't open#

    Windows opens the sheet on the screen your pointer is on. If you see Couldn't share, the file may have been moved or deleted since the capture; take it again or find it in the strip. The sheet is part of Windows 10 and 11, so there is nothing to install.
