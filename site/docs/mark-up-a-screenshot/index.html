    Manual · Editor

    Mark up a screenshot

    Open a capture in the editor and add an arrow, a box, some text, a highlight, a blur or a crop, then send it.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.3.5

    The editor is deliberately small. It has seven tools and nothing more, so marking up a screenshot takes a few seconds. Everything you draw stays editable until you save.

    Open a capture in the editor#

    Pick whichever is closest:

    • In the strip, select a capture and press E, double-click it, or right-click it and choose Edit.
    • Click Edit on the note that appears after a capture.
    • On the Home window, click one of the six captures, or click Editor to open the newest capture.
    • Turn on Open the editor after every capture in Strip and editor to open it automatically.

    Draw something#

    1. Choose a tool from the toolbar, or press its key.
    2. Drag on the picture. A line under the picture tells you what the tool does.
    3. Pick a colour and a width in the toolbar. They apply to what you draw next.
    4. Keep going, then press Ctrl+S or click Done to save. See Saving, Save as new and Revert.

    The colours are Nine Red, Volt, Ink, White and Blue. The widths are Thin, Medium and Thick. The highlighter starts out Volt.

    The tools#

    ToolKeyWhat it does
    SelectVClick a shape to select it, drag to move it, drag its handles to resize it. Change its colour or width, or press Delete to remove it.
    ArrowADrag from the tail to where the head should be. The head is where you let go.
    BoxBDrag out a rectangle outline.
    EllipseEDrag out an oval outline.
    HighlightHDrag over what matters. It paints a translucent band, Volt by default.
    TextTClick, type, then press Enter to finish. Shift+Enter starts a new line. Esc cancels. The width sets the text size.
    BlurUDrag over what to hide. It turns the area into flat blocks.
    CropCDrag the region to keep, adjust it with the handles, then press Enter. Esc cancels.

    To fix a text, double-click it. Text uses Geist with a soft dark outline, so it reads on any background.

    Blur#

    Blur pixelates for real. The saved file holds flat blocks where the area was, not the picture with a layer on top, so nobody can lift the blur off later. Before you save, undo still brings the pixels back. After you save, see the note on originals.

    Crop#

    Drag a region, then press Enter to apply it. The footer shows the new size and the original size. Undo brings the whole picture back until you save.

    Copy text#

    Click Copy text in the top bar to recognize the picture currently on screen with Windows' built-in OCR and put the words on the clipboard as plain text. This includes an unsaved crop or annotation, so what you see is what gets recognized. The editor stays open, your document keeps its unsaved state, and no file is written. A9 Snap says Text copied, or No text found when there was nothing to read.

    Undo, redo and zoom#

    • Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. They go back through the whole session, not just the last shape.
    • Ctrl+scroll zooms around the pointer. Ctrl+0 shows the picture at 100%. The zoom button in the top bar switches between fit and 100%.
    • Scroll to move around a zoomed picture. You can also hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle button.
