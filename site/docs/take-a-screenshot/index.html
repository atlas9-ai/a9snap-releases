    Manual · Capture

    Take a screenshot

    Press Alt+Z, drag a box, let go. The picture is on your clipboard and saved as a numbered file.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.3.3

    Use this whenever you want a picture of part of your screen. It works from any app, and it works on every monitor you have.

    Capture a region#

    1. Press Alt+Z. Every monitor freezes and dims. A crosshair, a small bullseye and a magnifier follow your pointer.
    2. Move to one corner of what you want.
    3. Press the mouse button and drag to the opposite corner. The box stays bright. Four Volt corner marks frame it, and a readout shows its size, such as 640 × 220.
    4. Let go. The picture is on your clipboard and saved as the next numbered file in your screenshots folder.

    A note appears at the bottom right of the monitor you captured, for example Copied · snag_0042.png. It goes away by itself after a moment. If you turn the confirmation off in Notifications, problems still show.

    Land on the exact pixel#

    The magnifier is a circle that shows the pixels under your pointer at 8 times their size, with a thin grid. The crosshair sits on the edge between pixels, so the sides of your box fall on pixel edges. The magnifier also shows the pointer's position in pixels. It moves to the other side of the pointer when you get near an edge of the screen.

    Tip: press and hold at the first corner, look at the magnifier, and nudge the pointer until the corner is exactly where you want it. Then drag.

    Cancel#

    You can cancel any of these ways. Nothing is copied or saved.

    • Press Esc.
    • Right-click.
    • Click without dragging, or drag only a few pixels. A tiny box counts as a click, so an accidental Alt+Z costs nothing.

    Several monitors#

    A9 Snap freezes all your monitors at their real resolution, so a screenshot is sharp even with display scaling at 125% or 150%. The crosshair, bullseye and magnifier are drawn only on the monitor your pointer is on. A capture comes from one monitor: the one where you press the mouse button. If you drag past its edge, the box stops at the edge.

    After you let go#

    • The clipboard holds the picture. Paste it with Ctrl+V into most apps. If an app wants a file, turn on Also copy as file in Saving.
    • The file is saved as the next number in your screenshots folder. See Where screenshots go.
    • The note is a button. Click Edit on it to draw on the picture. Click the note itself to open the recent captures strip with this capture selected. Hold Shift and click it to show the file in its folder.

    To open the editor after every capture without clicking, turn on Open the editor after every capture in Strip and editor.

    If the hotkey does nothing#

    Another app may own Alt+Z. A9 Snap tells you instead of failing quietly: a warning badge appears on the tray icon and Settings says Alt+Z is taken by another app. Pick another. Choose a different key in Shortcuts.
