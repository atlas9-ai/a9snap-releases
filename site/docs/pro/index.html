    Pro features

    Pro adds paid features to A9 Snap with one payment and no subscription. Everything you have today stays free. Here is what Pro includes, how to get it, enter a key, move it to another PC, and what it sends.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.7.1

    A9 Snap stays free: the hotkey, the picker, the strip, the editor, OCR and Start at login need no account and no license. Pro is for the extra features. It costs $14.99, once. It works on up to 3 of your PCs and every update is included. There is a 14-day money-back guarantee: write to the address in your purchase email and the payment is refunded.

    Open Pro features in the Home window's menu. With Pro on, the top card says Pro license activated, shows how many of your 3 PCs the key is on, and has Deactivate this PC. Without it, the card says A9 Snap Pro and has Get Pro, $14.99, Enter key and Restore purchase.

    What Pro includes#

    Only Secure capture is in A9 Snap today. With Pro on, its row on the Pro features page says how to use it: press Alt+Z, then S, or use Secure capture in the sidebar, with a How to use link to its manual page.

    When you use a Pro feature without Pro, A9 Snap shows a small sheet: "{Feature} is part of A9 Snap Pro", with Get Pro, $14.99, Enter key and Not now. Not now closes it and nothing else happens.

    Get Pro#

    1. Press Get Pro, $14.99. Your browser opens the checkout. The payment is handled by Stripe; A9 Snap never sees your card.
    2. Pay. A9 Snap waits for the payment and turns Pro on by itself, with no restart. The Pro features page says Finish paying in your browser while it waits, for up to 15 minutes. Stop waiting ends it.
    3. A key arrives by email as well. Keep it: you need it to put Pro on another PC.
    4. The first time Pro is on, the Home window says Secure capture is ready. Press Alt+Z, then S. with a Try it button, and the picker's key hint comes back for your next 5 captures.

    If the page says checkout isn't available, try again a little later.

    Enter a key#

    Open Pro features, paste the key from your purchase email into Have a key? and press Enter key. Pro turns on at once. The key looks like A9-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, and capitals, spaces and missing dashes don't matter.

    If the key is already on three PCs, A9 Snap shows the license server's message as it is. To use the key here, press Deactivate this PC on one of the others, or write to the address in your purchase email.

    Restore a purchase#

    Paid from this PC and Pro hasn't turned on, or you reinstalled Windows? Press Restore purchase. A9 Snap asks the license server whether a payment made from this PC went through and takes the key without you typing it. If nothing is found, use Enter key with the key from your email, which was sent when you paid.

    Moving Pro to another PC#

    On the PC that has it, open Pro features and press Deactivate this PC. That PC goes back to Free and the seat is free again. Then enter the key on the new PC.

    Refunds#

    A refunded payment turns the key off. A9 Snap finds out at its next check, which is when it starts and once a day, and then shows Free with a line saying why. Your captures are untouched.

    The license check#

    Free never makes a license check. Once a key is on your PC, A9 Snap asks the license server whether it is still good when it starts and then once a day, never in the middle of a capture. If your PC is offline, Pro keeps working for 30 days from the last good answer, and then waits for a connection. See Privacy for exactly what is sent.

    Where the key is kept#

    In pro-license.json in %APPDATA%\ai.atlas9.a9snap, beside your settings (the Microsoft Store version keeps its settings in its own folder). It holds the key, the license server's last answer and a time stamp. Delete the file and the PC is Free again; the seat stays used until you deactivate it or write to us.
