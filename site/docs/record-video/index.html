    Record video and GIF

    Record a window, a region or a whole screen as an MP4, then paste it like a picture. A short recording can become a GIF. It is a Pro feature.

    Last updated for A9 Snap 0.10.0

    Some things are easier to show than to screenshot: a click path, a bug that only happens when you hover. Video and GIF capture records what you choose to a small MP4 file, puts the file on the clipboard so it pastes into a chat or an email as an attachment, and keeps it next to your screenshots. It is part of A9 Snap Pro: see Pro features.

    Record something#

    1. Press Alt+Z.
    2. Press R. The size says Record, for example Record · 1280 × 720. (You can also press R after you start dragging, or just before you let go.)
    3. Click a window, press F for the whole monitor, or drag a region, as you always do.

    Recording starts the moment you let go. If you changed the capture hotkey, the keys follow it: your hotkey, then R. The picker's hint line says R record (Pro). R is never sticky: the next capture starts as a normal picture.

    R and S (secure capture) do not go together in this version. Press one while the other is on and the picker says so.

    Without Pro, R does not record. The picker says Video capture is part of A9 Snap Pro, the capture is a normal picture, and the Pro sheet opens once after the picker closes.

    While it records#

    A small bar sits just outside the area you chose, on the same monitor (inside its bottom edge when the area fills the screen). It has a Volt dot, the time, and:

    • Pause and Resume. Paused time is left out of the video.
    • Stop. Or press Alt+Z again, whichever window has the keyboard.
    • The microphone and PC sound switches. Both are off until you turn them on, and A9 Snap remembers your choice. PC sound is whatever your speakers are playing.

    A thin Volt outline marks the recorded area. Neither the outline nor the bar is ever in the video. Press Esc while the bar has the keyboard (or while the pointer is on it) and it asks Discard recording?, with Discard and Keep recording.

    A recording stops by itself after 10 minutes, with a warning 10 seconds before (Stops in 9 s on the bar). Change that under Longest recording in Settings: 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes.

    What you get#

    • A file, in the same numbering as your pictures. The next number in your screenshots folder, ending in .mp4: if the last picture was snag_0042.png, the recording is snag_0043.mp4, and the next picture is snag_0044.png. See Where screenshots go.
    • On the clipboard as a file, so Ctrl+V attaches it in a chat or an email.
    • A toast that says Recording saved · copied as file, with Save as GIF (for a recording up to 30 seconds) and Show in folder. Clicking the toast opens the strip on it.

    The video is H.264 at 30 frames a second, made with your graphics card's encoder when it has one. If the area is taller than 1080 pixels it is scaled down to 1080 lines, keeping its shape. The mouse pointer is in the video (turn that off with Show the pointer in recordings in Settings).

    Make a GIF#

    Press Save as GIF on the toast, or right-click the recording in the strip and choose Save as GIF. A9 Snap writes a GIF next to the recording with the next number, copies it as a file and says so. It is 15 frames a second, never wider than 960 pixels, with a palette chosen for that clip. A recording longer than 30 seconds can't become a GIF. A longer clip takes longer to convert, because every frame has to be decoded twice (once to choose the palette): a few seconds for a short region on a typical PC, and a minute or more for 30 seconds of a full 1080p screen on a slow one. Making the GIF… stays on screen until it is done.

    In the strip and the Home window#

    A recording's card shows its first frame, a play badge and its length. Press Enter or double-click to play it in your default player. Ctrl+C, dragging it out, Delete and Undo work as they do for a picture; Copy puts the file on the clipboard. Search finds a recording by its name and by the app and window title noted when it started. The editor does not open recordings, and Secure capture, Auto-redact and search inside pictures do not apply to them in this version.

    Settings#

    Open Saving in the Home window.

    • Show the pointer in recordings. On by default.
    • Longest recording. 1, 5, 10 (the default) or 30 minutes.

    The microphone and PC sound switches are on the recording bar.

    Privacy#

    Recording happens on your PC and the files stay there: A9 Snap never uploads a recording. Sound is recorded only when you turn it on: the microphone and PC sound are off by default, and switching one off closes it. The log says how long a recording was and its size, never what was on screen. See Privacy.

    If it doesn't work#

    • "The screen sent no picture to record." A locked PC or a protected screen has nothing to capture. Unlock it and try again.
    • A sound switch turns itself off and the bar names the problem: no microphone, no sound output, or Windows blocking the microphone (Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone, which has to allow desktop apps).
    • A recording that was running when you quit A9 Snap is finished and saved before it closes.
